# Code of conduct

External Signer is security-sensitive open-source software. Discussion should
help people understand, test and improve it without making the project unsafe
or needlessly hostile.

## Expected behaviour

- Be clear, constructive and specific.
- Challenge code, claims and decisions rather than attacking people.
- Respect different levels of experience and ask before assuming intent.
- Keep examples and reports free of identity keys, pairing capabilities,
  account data and private infrastructure details.
- Correct mistakes openly and give credit for useful work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or targeted personal abuse.
- Publishing another person's private information or credentials.
- Pressuring anyone to disclose an `nsec`, pairing URI or other secret.
- Deliberately misleading users about custody, permissions, publication,
  settlement or production evidence.
- Disrupting project spaces or repeatedly ignoring maintainer direction.

## Enforcement

The Crypto Donkey maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments,
issues or other participation that breaches this policy. Repeated or serious
breaches may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Use GitHub's private reporting and moderation tools when a report should not be
public. Never put a vulnerability or live secret in a conduct report or public
issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security disclosures.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project.
